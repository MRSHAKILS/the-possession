Why no API can do this

A closed model gives you text in and text out. Possession happens in the place between: the 3,584 numbers each layer hands to the next. To reach them you need the weights on a machine you control.

  1. Extract. Run the model on paired prompts where only the speaker differs: a ghost or an ordinary person. The average difference in activations, at each layer, is the ghost.
  2. Possess. Add that direction to layers 16 to 21 while the model generates.
  3. Exorcise. In layers 22 to 25, measure how far activations sit along the ghost direction, and remove the excess. It needs no knowledge of how strong the possession was.
